# Quality and Safety Checklist

Run this review before delivering publish-ready content.

## Editorial quality
- [ ] The post has one clear objective.
- [ ] The opening is relevant and truthful.
- [ ] The body provides useful context or value.
- [ ] The text is easy to scan on a phone.
- [ ] The CTA is clear and appropriate.
- [ ] The language and tone match the audience.
- [ ] No unnecessary repetition, hashtag stuffing, or emoji overload.
- [ ] The draft can be understood without hidden context.

## Fact check
- [ ] Product names and features match supplied information.
- [ ] Price, discount, and availability were not invented.
- [ ] Dates, times, locations, delivery, and contact details are confirmed.
- [ ] Claims, statistics, awards, and certifications have evidence.
- [ ] Testimonials are genuine and not altered to exaggerate.
- [ ] Assumptions or placeholders are visible.

## Trust and safety
- [ ] No fake urgency or false scarcity.
- [ ] No fabricated review, endorsement, customer story, or result.
- [ ] No guaranteed financial, medical, legal, or business outcome.
- [ ] No misleading before/after representation.
- [ ] No discriminatory, harassing, or humiliating language.
- [ ] No personal or sensitive information is exposed unnecessarily.
- [ ] No copyrighted copy is reproduced without permission.
- [ ] No spam tactics or manipulative engagement bait.

## Platform and legal review

This Skill does not replace a current Meta policy or legal review. For regulated, restricted, sensitive, or commerce-related categories, check the current Meta rules and relevant local laws before publishing. Escalate uncertainty rather than guessing.

## Rating
- **Ready:** all critical checks pass.
- **Needs confirmation:** a missing fact or condition is marked.
- **Revise:** misleading, unsafe, spam-like, or unsupported content is present.

When revising, briefly state the material issue and provide a safer alternative.
