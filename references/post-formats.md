# Facebook Post Formats

Use the format that fits the audience and objective. These are structures, not rigid formulas.

## 1. Educational / tip post
1. Specific problem or question.
2. One-sentence answer.
3. Three to five useful tips or steps.
4. Short closing insight.
5. CTA inviting a relevant question or next step.

Template:
~~~
[പ്രശ്നം / ചോദ്യം]

ഇത് ചെയ്യാൻ സഹായിക്കുന്ന [number] കാര്യങ്ങൾ:
1. [പ്രായോഗിക നിർദ്ദേശം]
2. [പ്രായോഗിക നിർദ്ദേശം]
3. [പ്രായോഗിക നിർദ്ദേശം]

[ചുരുക്കത്തിലുള്ള takeaway]

നിങ്ങൾക്ക് ഇതിൽ ഏതാണ് കൂടുതൽ പ്രയോജനപ്പെട്ടത്?
~~~

## 2. Product promotion
1. Audience need or use case.
2. Product and verified features.
3. Practical benefit.
4. Confirmed price/offer/availability, if supplied.
5. Clear buyer action.

Template:
~~~
[ഉപഭോക്താവിന്റെ ആവശ്യം / use case] ഉണ്ടോ?

[Product name] — [verified key feature].

ഇതിന്റെ പ്രധാന ഉപയോഗങ്ങൾ:
• [Verified benefit 1]
• [Verified benefit 2]
• [Verified benefit 3]

വില: [confirmed price / not supplied]
ലഭ്യത: [confirmed availability / not supplied]

കൂടുതൽ വിവരങ്ങൾക്ക് [confirmed contact/action].
~~~

Never turn a feature into an unsupported performance claim. Do not insert an invented discount or deadline.

## 3. Brand story
1. A real moment, challenge, or motivation supplied by the user.
2. What happened.
3. What was learned.
4. Why it matters to the audience.
5. Gentle CTA.

Never invent a founder story or claim personal experience that was not supplied.

## 4. Engagement question
1. Familiar, relevant context.
2. One simple question.
3. Optional answer choices.
4. Friendly invitation.

Avoid irrelevant "comment YES" or "tag 10 friends" engagement bait.

## 5. Announcement
- What is new?
- Who is it for?
- When does it take effect? (only if supplied)
- What should the audience do next?

Put dates, location, eligibility, and contact details in a scannable block when relevant.

## 6. Offer / campaign post
1. Clear offer headline.
2. Eligible product/service.
3. Exact discount, price, or benefit.
4. Start/end date and conditions if provided.
5. How to redeem.
6. CTA.

If a condition is unknown, mark it for confirmation. Never create false urgency or scarcity.

## 7. Local business post
Use local references only when genuine and useful:
- Service area or locality.
- What is available.
- Who benefits.
- Confirmed contact or visit details.

Do not invent an address or claim "near you" without a known location.

## 8. Testimonial / social proof
Use genuine customer words or an accurate, permission-aware summary.
- Preserve the meaning.
- Do not embellish results.
- Do not change a neutral comment into a strong endorsement.
- Remove personal contact details unless the customer intended them to be public.

## 9. Marketplace product listing

Title: [Product type] + [important feature] + [size/variant if known]

Description:
- Product: [name/type]
- Condition: [new/used/other — confirmed]
- Size/colour/material: [confirmed details]
- Included items: [confirmed details]
- Price: [confirmed price]
- Location/delivery: [confirmed terms]
- Known limitations/defects: [disclose honestly]
- Contact: [confirmed buyer action]

Avoid keyword stuffing, unrelated brands, misleading photos, hidden defects, or bait prices.

## 10. Reel / short video
Deliver:
- Hook (opening moment).
- Scene 1–4 (visual + spoken line or overlay).
- End frame.
- Caption.
- CTA.

Show the real product or service accurately. Keep the script natural to speak aloud.

## 11. Carousel / album
Possible narratives:
- Problem → why it happens → steps → summary.
- Myth → fact → explanation → action.
- Product need → feature → benefit → details → CTA.
- Story → turning point → lesson → question.

One key thought per slide. Use a short title and minimal body text. Provide the caption separately.
