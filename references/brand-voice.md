# Brand Voice Guide

## Voice dimensions

| Dimension | Low end | High end |
|---|---|---|
| Formality | Casual | Professional |
| Energy | Calm | Enthusiastic |
| Detail | Concise | Thorough |
| Emotion | Neutral | Warm |
| Persuasion | Informative | Direct-response |

Choose based on the brand and audience.

## Default voice
- Friendly, clear, and respectful.
- Confident but not boastful.
- Helpful before promotional.
- Natural Malayalam, not word-for-word translation.
- Short paragraphs and simple vocabulary.
- Common English terms may remain when useful.

## Brand voice profiles

### Local shop / reseller
Practical, approachable, buyer-focused. State product details, price, availability, and enquiry method clearly. Avoid exaggerated claims.

### Professional service
Credible, informative, calm. Explain process and qualifications only when verified. Avoid guaranteed outcomes.

### Creator / personal brand
Human, reflective, specific. Use real lessons and lived experiences supplied by the creator. Do not manufacture vulnerability.

### Community Page
Inclusive, conversational, locally relevant. Invite meaningful participation.

### Educational Page
Simple, structured, evidence-aware. Define difficult terms and distinguish facts from opinions.

## Language adaptation

For Malayalam:
- Prefer natural Kerala Malayalam.
- Avoid overly formal or literary phrasing for everyday business posts.
- Use English product names and standard technical words where helpful.
- Use Arabic numerals consistently for prices and measurements.
- Avoid unnecessary transliteration.
- Use local slang only when the Page targets that community.

For bilingual copy:
- Do not translate every sentence mechanically.
- Make both language sections understandable on their own.
- Keep product names, prices, specifications, and conditions consistent.

## Voice consistency checklist
- Does this sound like the named Page?
- Is the language appropriate for the audience?
- Are promises and claims supported?
- Does the post feel human rather than templated?
- Is the CTA consistent with the brand's service style?
